Japanese Radio

A free iOS app for listening to short Japanese conversations. ハナ and ケン talk through one topic an episode. The speech is synthesized with VOICEVOX, and the scripts are written with AI assistance.

These pages are the privacy policy, terms, and support contact for the app. The site is static. It does not use cookies, accounts, or analytics.
