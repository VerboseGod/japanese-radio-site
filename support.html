FAQ

Is the app free?
Yes. This version has no account, no ads, and no purchases.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The sample episodes are inside the app. Playback does not need a network. Download keeps another copy in the app's storage. Furigana, English, and playback speed are remembered on the device.
Where are the voice credits?
Open the Credits tab. The current voices are VOICEVOX:冥鳴ひまり and VOICEVOX:青山龍星. Speech is synthesized with VOICEVOX (https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/). The hosts ハナ and ケン are fictional. The same screen links to the privacy policy and the terms of use.
A Japanese line sounds wrong.
Email the episode title and the line, in Japanese or in English. The scripts are AI-assisted learning conversations, not a news report and not a graded exam answer.
What data do you have about me?
From the app, none. Listening history and settings stay on the device. Read the privacy policy for TestFlight, App Store crash reports, and email. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. If you want an email thread deleted, ask in a reply to that thread.
I am using TestFlight.
TestFlight is Apple's beta install. Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with me. That is separate from the app, which has no analytics SDK. Details are in the privacy policy.
Does CarPlay work?
CarPlay does not connect in the current build. The audio entitlement was requested from Apple on October 27, 2026 and is not turned on yet. Until it is granted and a build ships with it, use the phone. Lock-screen play, pause, skip back 15 seconds, and the playback speeds 0.75, 0.9, and 1.0 still work.
Is this for children?
It is a general language-learning app. It is not directed at children under 13. There is no account.
