Privacy policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy describes the Japanese Radio iOS app (bundle id com.verbosegod.japaneseradio) and this website. I am Alexey Zelmanovich, an individual in Israel, and I publish the app. "I" and "me" mean me. The Privacy policy link in the app opens this page.

Summary

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or an account, and it does not send your listening or your settings to me. There is no sign-in and no advertising identifier. What the app remembers stays on your device. I do not sell personal information, and the app does not build a profile of you.

Apple can still collect crash and diagnostic data when you use TestFlight or the App Store. That is described below. If you email me, I receive that email.

Information that stays on your device

The app stores these in its own storage on the phone or tablet:

  • Whether furigana and the English translation are shown
  • Playback speed
  • A short list of episodes you have already played: the episode id, the station id, the title, and the time you played it
  • Episodes you download, including the audio and the transcript file
  • In a development build only, a program-guide address, if one was saved

I do not have a copy of that list. Deleting the app removes it from the device. If you use iCloud or a computer backup, Apple's backup may include this on-device data. I do not control that backup.

The lock screen and Control Center can show the episode title, the station name, and the hosts' names so you can press play and pause. The app gives that information to iOS on the device. It is not sent to me.

Listening

The episodes in the current app are bundled inside the app. Playing them, offline or in the background, does not contact a server that I operate. I do not operate a content server for this version.

A development build can save a program-guide address and then download feed.json, audio, and a transcript from that address. The App Store build does not show a field for that address. A download like that is an ordinary file request. The app does not add your name, an account, or an advertising identifier to it.

How episodes are made

The speech is text-to-speech made with VOICEVOX before the episode is put in the app. The hosts ハナ and ケン are fictional. The current scripts are AI-assisted unless an episode's own record says otherwise. That work happens while the episode is produced, not while you listen. The app does not send your listening, your settings, or a script to an AI service or a speech service.

A later version may add a service that generates an episode while you use the app. That service is not in this version. Before any version sends a topic, a script, a voice request, or anything about you to a server or an AI provider, I will update this policy to say what is sent, who receives it, why, and how long it is kept. The Privacy policy link in the app opens this page.

Apple, TestFlight, and the App Store

The app does not include a crash reporter or an analytics SDK.

If you install the app from TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with me. For an email invitation, that can include the name and email address on the invitation. Feedback you send in TestFlight is shared with me as well. Apple's TestFlight privacy notice says testers cannot opt out of that collection: TestFlight & Privacy.

If you install the app from the App Store, Apple may collect diagnostics. When Share with App Developers is on, under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash data and statistics with me. That sharing is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

I use crash information to fix the app. I do not combine it with an advertising profile, and I do not sell it.

The app does not use Apple's advertising identifier and does not ask to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you use Siri or Shortcuts to start playback, Apple handles the voice request. The app then plays on the device. I do not receive a recording of that request.

This website

These pages are static files. They do not set cookies, do not run analytics, and do not ask you to create an account. Opening a page does not tell me who you are. Your browser still contacts the host that serves the files, such as GitHub Pages, under that host's own policy.

Children

Japanese Radio is a general language-learning app. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account and nowhere to enter a name, an email address, or an age. If you believe a child has emailed me personal information, write to me and I will delete that message.

Email

If you write to alexey.zelman@gmail.com, I use the message to answer you and to fix a problem you report. The inbox is Gmail, so Google processes that mail under Google's terms. I keep the thread while I need it to handle the conversation, and I delete it when I no longer do. I do not add you to a mailing list. There is no account, so I cannot email you about the app unless you wrote to me first.

Changes

When this policy changes, I publish the new text on this page and change the effective date at the top. The Privacy policy link in the app opens this page, so the next time you open the link you see the current text. If a change is material and an update of the app depends on it, that update's App Store "What's New" text will say that the policy changed. I have no mailing list, so I cannot email every listener. If you keep using the app after the new effective date, the updated policy applies to that use.
